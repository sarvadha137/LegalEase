RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is between Arun Kumar (Tenant) and Priya Sharma (Landlord).

The tenant agrees to pay a monthly rent of ₹15,000.
The rent must be paid on or before the 5th day of every month.

The agreement begins on 1 October 2026 and continues for a period of 11 months.

The tenant must maintain the property in good condition.
The tenant shall provide 30 days written notice before vacating the property.

The security deposit is ₹30,000 and will be returned after the tenant vacates the property, subject to deductions for damages or unpaid dues.

Both parties agree to follow the terms stated in this agreement.
